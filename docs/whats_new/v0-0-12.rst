v0.0.12 (Unreleased)
++++++++++++++++++++++

New Features
############
- The exergoeconomic analysis runs without split physical exergy as well. A material stream then
  carries a single cost rate for its physical exergy, ``C^PH``, instead of one for its thermal and
  one for its mechanical exergy, and the auxiliary equations of every component follow. Models that
  cannot provide the split, such as the ones exported from Aspen Plus, can be costed now. The split
  resolves the fuel and the product of a component more finely, so the specific costs of the two
  cases differ, and below the ambient temperature a component either keeps the cold exergy in its
  outlet stream or turns dissipative, with a warning that names it.
- The cost columns of the connection results follow the cost variables of the analysis: ``C^T`` and
  ``C^M`` with split physical exergy, ``C^PH`` without.

Bug Fixes
#########
- The ``c^TOT`` column of a heat or power stream in the table of the material connections was filled
  with the value of ``C^TOT``.

API Changes
###########
- ``aux_eqs()``, ``dis_eqs()`` and ``exergoeconomic_balance()`` of a component take an additional
  ``split_physical_exergy`` argument. Custom components have to accept it; it defaults to ``True``,
  so an implementation that ignores it keeps working as long as the split is used.

Testing
#######
- Every component is checked against the counting invariant of the cost matrix: it has to provide one
  auxiliary equation less than it has cost variables on its outlet streams (one more than that, and
  no cost balance, when it is dissipative). The check runs over three models, with and without split
  physical exergy and with and without chemical exergy.
